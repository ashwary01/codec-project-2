How This NLP Chatbot Engine Works

1

Text Preprocessing & Normalization

Converts incoming raw input into lowercase, strips special characters/punctuations, tokenizes sentences into individual word tokens, removes English stopwords (e.g. "is", "the", "a", "my"), and applies Porter Stemming to reduce words to their root stems (e.g. "tracking" → "track").

2

Feature Extraction (TF-IDF & N-Grams)

Computes Term Frequency - Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF) vectors for both the user input and training intent phrases. Words that are rare across intent documents carry higher discriminatory weight in the vector space model.

3

Similarity & Intent Classification

Calculates the Cosine Similarity score (the cosine of the angle between two word vectors in multi-dimensional space):

$$\text{Cosine Similarity} = \frac{\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B}}{\|\vec{A}\| \|\vec{B}\|}$$

The intent with the highest matching confidence percentage is selected as the top predicted intent.

4

Named Entity Recognition (NER) & Regex

Scans the text using regex rules to dynamically detect and extract key entities such as Order IDs (e.g., ORD-98234), Email addresses, Dates, Dollar amounts, and Product Names.

5

Sentiment Analysis & Escalation

Scans for emotion lexicon words (positive vs frustrated/negative). If frustration level exceeds a threshold or consecutive fallbacks occur, the chatbot automatically initiates an automated human representative escalation ticket!

6

Stateful Multi-Turn Context

Remembers conversation context (such as pending order verification) across multiple message turns to prompt users naturally for missing details.
